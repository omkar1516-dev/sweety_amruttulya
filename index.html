Our Story

Sweety Amruttulya (स्विटी अमृततुल्य) was born out of a passion for traditional tea craftsmanship. "Amruttulya" literally translates to "As sweet and pure as Nectar".

We have perfected a unique blend of high-grade tea leaves, aromatic spices, and natural sweetness that delivers consistent, restaurant-quality tea every single time, in just minutes.

चाय बनाने की विधि (Brewing Process)

How to make the perfect cup of Sweety Amruttulya Tea

1

Step 1: Heat Water

Take 100ml of fresh water in a tea vessel. Bring it to a boil on medium flame.

2

Step 2: Add Sweety Amruttulya Premix

Add 14g to 15g (approx. 1 full tablespoon) of Sweety Amruttulya Premix Powder into the boiling mixture.

3

Step 3: Simmer & Boil

Allow it to simmer for 3 minutes so that the aromatic spices and tea extracts infuse completely.

4

Step 4: Strain & Enjoy

Pour through a tea strainer into your cup. Your hot, flavorful Sweety Amruttulya Tea is ready! "हर घूँट में मिठास"
